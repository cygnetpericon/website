# CRRS Roadmap

> Direction, not a fixed schedule.

**A note on this page:** this describes the direction CRRS is evolving in, guided by the platform's architecture and philosophy. It intentionally does not commit to specific dates or feature lists — those belong in release notes and delivery plans maintained separately from this page. This section should be reviewed and filled in with confirmed commitments before publication.

---

# Guiding Direction

CRRS's roadmap follows directly from the platform principles it is built on: knowledge before execution, and metadata over hard-coded logic. As those principles get applied more broadly, CRRS is expected to extend along a few consistent directions.

---

# Broader Regulatory Coverage

CRRS's current scope spans specific reporting domains — see [CRRS](/products/crrs) for the current list of supported integrations. Because CRRS is built on CRRR, RRDF, and CBDF rather than domain-specific code, extending coverage to new regulatory reporting obligations is expected to be a matter of extending the platform's knowledge base rather than rebuilding the application.

---

# Deeper Automation

As CRTP's regression testing coverage matures alongside CRRS, the platform is expected to support more frequent, lower-risk regulatory updates — reducing the manual verification burden that traditionally accompanies regulatory change.

---

# Expanded AI Assistance

The platform's architecture — structured, governed regulatory knowledge in CRRR — is designed to support AI-assisted capabilities such as regulation interpretation, impact analysis, and data quality investigation. CRRS is expected to surface these capabilities to its users as they mature at the platform level.

---

# Beyond Regulatory Reporting

Because CBDF's canonical model extends beyond regulatory reporting, the underlying platform is capable of supporting adjacent use cases — risk management, internal controls, data governance — beyond CRRS's current reporting focus. Whether and when these become part of CRRS specifically is a product decision to be made separately.
